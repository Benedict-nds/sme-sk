# Client needs brief

The brief turns the source comparison into a page the team can read in two minutes. It describes the client's situation and problems. It does not recommend solutions, because that is the next stage. Follow the `house-rules` skill throughout.

## Format

Write `work/needs-brief.md` with these sections, in this order, keeping the whole thing to roughly one page (about 400 words):

1. **Business snapshot.** What they do, rough size, how they operate, and the tools they use today. Each point has a source.
2. **Problems, in their own words.** The main problems, each with a short quote or close paraphrase and its source. Order by how strongly the client stressed them.
3. **What they asked for.** The changes the client says they want.
4. **What they may actually need.** Where the real problem looks different from the request. Mark these "(inference)" and say what evidence points that way.
5. **Constraints.** Budget, staff skills, connectivity, timeline, and who decides, as far as the sources say.
6. **Conflicts and gaps.** Carried over from the source comparison.
7. **Verify with client.** The short list of things to confirm.

## One thing to watch

Clients often describe a solution instead of a problem ("we need an app"). Record the solution they named in section 3 and look for the problem underneath it for section 2. Keeping those apart is what lets the next stage see when the request and the need don't match.
